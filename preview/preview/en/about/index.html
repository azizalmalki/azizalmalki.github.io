About Aziz Almalki

I have presented social and cultural programmes on screen and hosted events in front of live audiences, and I currently lead a media and marketing team in Qatar.

In brief

Aziz Almalki is an Algerian media professional, TV presenter and event host working in Qatar. On television he presented the social programme Hadith Al-Shari and the cultural programme Awtad Al-Khiyam; he co-founded Lamrda, a weekly programme on poetry and storytelling, and he hosts events in front of live audiences. He started on camera in 2022, has worked in journalism, and supervised video production during his university studies. He currently leads the media and marketing team at Sahari Group in Qatar. He holds a Licence in Quran and Sunnah Studies from the University of Oran 1 Ahmed Ben Bella and trained in digital content creation with BBC Media Action.

About Me

I started on camera in 2022, then co-founded Lamrda, a weekly programme about poetry and storytelling.

On television I presented Hadith Al-Shari, which carries people's voices as they are, and Awtad Al-Khiyam, on Hassaniya culture. On stage, I host events in front of live audiences.

Today I work in Qatar, leading the media and marketing team at Sahari Group. I present, interview, write, film and edit, and I look for work that puts me in front of an audience, on camera and on stage.
